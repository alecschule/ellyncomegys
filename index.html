About Ellyn

Ellyn has been a Registered Nurse for 20+ years and has been able to intertwine her nursing experience and her interest in healing herself and others into her healing business. Her passion is health and happiness for everyone.

Ellyn has a Master’s Degree in counseling, completed a 2-year Gestalt training, certified as an art therapist and transformational coach. She also has completed the first level of Reiki and has received the certification to be a Conscious Connected Healer.
